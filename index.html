Got Questions?

Crazy Taxi PC Modding & Retro Game FAQ

Answers to the most common questions regarding Crazy Taxi PC mods, original soundtrack restoration, Dreamcast emulation, and game modding guides.

01 How do I mod Crazy Taxi on PC? ▼

Modding Crazy Taxi on PC is 100% plug-and-play with LFM (Crazy Taxi 1 MOD NEW). Simply download the package (Lyras Framwork MOD.7z), extract dinput8.dll and the mods/ directory directly into your root game directory where Crazy Taxi.exe is located, and launch the game! No installers, registry modifications, or file replacements are required. Press Shift+F1 in-game to open the mod menu overlay.

02 How to restore the Offspring music in Crazy Taxi? ▼

The original arcade and Sega Dreamcast soundtrack by The Offspring ("All I Want", "Way Down the Line") and Bad Religion ("Ten in 2010", "The Meaning of Life") is restored automatically. When you place dinput8.dll in the game folder, Feature 09 hooks the native CRI ADX audio streaming engine at runtime, immediately replacing the generic re-release songs with the original high-fidelity arcade tracks.

03 Can you fly in Crazy Taxi? (Full 3D Taxi Flight Mode) ▼

Yes! Feature 19 introduces Full 3D Taxi Fly Mode. By pressing F (or toggling in the Shift+F1 Mod Menu), you bypass terrain collisions to soar through San Francisco skies. Press Space to climb and Shift or Ctrl to descend. A smart auto-landing routine temporarily engages during customer drop-off zones, automatically lifting you 8 meters back into the air so you can continue cruising through the sky seamlessly.

04 How to unlock all cabs in Crazy Taxi PC? ▼

In LFM v1.0, Feature 07 automatically writes unlock bitmasks to memory address 0x0164fa58 (0xFF) and 0x016aa3c0 (0xFFFFFFFF). This instantly unlocks all vehicle options, including the hidden Bike, the Sega Master Rickshaw, and all character special rides without requiring manual Crazy Box challenge completions.

05 Is the Crazy Taxi Steam version the same as Dreamcast? ▼

No, the Steam version differs substantially from the Dreamcast and Arcade original. The Steam version removed the licensed Offspring/Bad Religion soundtrack, replaced authentic commercial destinations (Pizza Hut, KFC, Tower Records, FILA) with generic fictional storefronts, lacks native 16:9/21:9 widescreen support, and suffers from controller deadzone issues. Our LFM: Crazy Taxi 1 MOD NEW (v1.0) suite completely restores all original content on Steam while adding 60 FPS smoothness, 2K texture upscaling, and 3D flight physics.

06 Is the Crazy Taxi mod safe? ▼

Yes, 100%. The LFM framework is an open-source C++ project licensed under Creative Commons CC BY-NC 4.0. It contains zero spyware, adware, telemetry, or third-party wrappers. Because it loads solely via Windows standard dynamic-link library redirection (dinput8.dll), it does not alter executable binaries or save files on your hard drive.

07 How to uninstall Crazy Taxi mods? ▼

Uninstalling is as simple as deleting dinput8.dll and the mods/ folder from your Crazy Taxi installation directory. Because the framework performs non-invasive runtime memory detours, your game returns to completely unmodified vanilla status instantly with zero leftover files.

08 What is Another Day mode in Crazy Taxi? ▼

Another Day is Sega's official alternate passenger layout built into the original arcade engine (memory flag 0x01662304). Enabling it rearranges all passenger spawn points and destination zones throughout the entire city, offering experienced players an entirely fresh, chaotic route challenge.

09 Is there a Crazy Taxi remaster? ▼

While SEGA has not released an official 4K/2K remaster, LFM: Crazy Taxi 1 MOD NEW (v1.0) serves as the definitive community fan remaster for PC. It brings real-time 2K HD texture replacement, chunk streaming fixes, 60 FPS unlocked physics, 16:9/21:9 ultrawide FOV matrices, full 3D flight dynamics, and 100% authentic Dreamcast audio & brand restorations to modern PCs.

10 How to get the original Crazy Taxi on PC? ▼

The best way to play the authentic arcade/Dreamcast experience on modern PC (Windows 10 & Windows 11) is by installing any legally owned PC version (e.g. the Steam release) and dropping the free LFM v1.0 mod package into the game folder. This effortlessly provides the restored original music, authentic real-world storefronts, 60 FPS fluidity, and clean modern gamepad mapping.

11 What is the Barbie Glamour Theme? ▼

The Barbie Glamour aesthetic is a custom visual mode in the Mod Menu (Shift+F1) under Settings & Persistence → Interface. It transforms the overlay with velvet berry backdrops, hot magenta headers, rose-gold accents, and matching vibrant pink HUD speedometer instruments.

12 Does this mod include widescreen, 60 FPS, and Windows 10/11 fixes? ▼

Yes. The framework fixes internal camera projection matrices to deliver true 16:9 and 21:9 ultrawide aspect ratios without horizontal stretching, locks a rock-solid 60 FPS, and includes signature scanning to ensure seamless execution on modern 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11 systems.
